Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

YouTube Shorts Publisher is a private, owner-operated automation tool.

Google and YouTube data

The application requests the minimum YouTube permission needed to upload videos: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It uses this access only to upload and manage videos belonging to the authorized owner’s YouTube channel.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and generated media are stored on the owner’s private server. Google user data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings. Contact the owner if you have questions.
